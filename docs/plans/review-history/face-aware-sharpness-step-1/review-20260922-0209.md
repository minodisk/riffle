## Round 1

**Reviewed commit**: `ae2cff82173b6467036bd54f62ade9f07e2b251b` feat(core): add a YuNet face and eye detector prototype with a CLI benchmark

STATUS: NEEDS_FIX

### Findings

1. **crates/cli/src/main.rs:113-153 (`faces`) and crates/core/src/faces.rs:80 (`detect`)**: Detection runs on the raw, un-rotated preview, and `apply_orientation` is applied only after drawing. For portrait frames (orientation 6/8), the faces the model sees lie on their side. YuNet is trained on upright faces, so recall on vertical shots drops sharply. The "left/right eye by x" ordering also becomes "top/bottom" in the displayed image. The `bench` row and the planned by-eye check on the Mac would therefore under-report misses on portrait frames. Fix: in `faces` (and `bench`), call `apply_orientation` before `faces::detect`, then draw on the oriented buffer. Also document in `detect`'s doc comment that the caller must pass an upright image, or accept an orientation argument. Record the choice in learnings.md so Step 2/3 inherit it.
