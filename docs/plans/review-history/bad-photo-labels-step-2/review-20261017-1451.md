3. **[docs/plans/20261017-bad-photo-labels/badfilter.md:192-194]**: The within-sample weighted cut for `B0` / `L0` ("remove the lowest-scored sampled frames until their `1/π` sum reaches `q`% of the folder's `1/π` sum") leaves the stopping point undefined: does it include the frame that crosses `q`% or stop before it? That choice matters a lot here, because strata 1–6 are drawn library-wide, so one cue-stratum frame can dominate a small folder's weight. Example: `2026-07-21` has 8 `rest` frames at 2.25 each plus 1 `focus_low` frame at about 71, so its `1/π` sum is about 89. If the `focus_low` frame scores lowest, an "include the crossing frame" rule removes about 80% of the folder's weight at `q` = 10, and a "stop before" rule removes nothing. The paired dedicated-model bar is read on exactly these cuts. Before labels exist, fix the rule (e.g. the shortest prefix whose `1/π` sum is >= `q`%, or the longest prefix whose sum is <= `q`%, with ties by the seed-0 draw). Also say that a folder's `1/π` sum is not its frame count.

4. **[docs/plans/20261017-bad-photo-labels/badfilter.md:247-249]** (with :110 and :224): The low-base-rate note says a base rate under 5% makes the `P` floors hard to reach. The stronger consequence, which follows from the arithmetic and should be written down now, is about `NL`. The smallest graded cut removes 10% of every folder's frames, so `NL` <= 5% requires `BR · b` >= 0.05 + 0.05·b. That is impossible when `b` is below about 5.3%, and it already needs `BR` >= 55% at `b` = 10%. So at a low or moderate base rate, no graded score can meet the worth-a-filter bar at any `q` cut, and the "primary reading at the cut whose `NL` is nearest 5%" is always `q` = 10. Only the binary rules and `G0`'s `quantile@95` cut would then be live candidates. Either add a smaller cut (e.g. `q` = 5) to the pre-listed grid while it can still change, updating Step 2's text in `plan.md` to match, or state this feasibility limit next to the base-rate note. That way a "no usable filter" outcome is not misread as evidence about the scores when the cut grid caused it.
