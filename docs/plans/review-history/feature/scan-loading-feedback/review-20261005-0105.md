### Findings

1. **crates/app/ui/src/main.ts:2746**: `setScanProgress("0%")` sits in `startScan()`, and `startScan()` is also the tail of every in-place `resync()`: `focus` (each window refocus past `FOCUS_RESCAN_INTERVAL`), `watch` (each debounced folder change), `reload`, `trash`, `restore` and `rename`. So an already-open, fully indexed folder shows the 0% track above the strip for the whole `scan_folder` listing + reconcile phase on every alt-tab back and every external file change. On a large folder on Windows, the folder the plan is about, that phase is not instant, and a 0% bar reads as "loading started" even though no new work is coming. The plan's agreed decision and its trade-off section accept the flash only for "a fully cached reopen" (`openDirectory`); resyncs are not mentioned, and they happen far more often. Suggested fix: move the `setScanProgress("0%")` call out of `startScan()` and into `openDirectory()`, just before its `void startScan(folder)` (after the existing `setScanProgress(null)` reset). A resync then shows the bar only when `scan-progress` reports real pass-1 work, and the `scan-done` / catch hides stay correct for both paths. If showing the bar on resync is what you want, record that as a deliberate trade-off in plan.md's "Trade-offs and risks" and the Step 1 Done-when, and note in the `startScan` comment that the bar shows on resyncs too.
