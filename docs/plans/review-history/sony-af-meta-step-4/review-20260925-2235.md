### Findings

1. **docs/usage.md:101**: "the AF tracking (`Face tracking` or `Lock On AF`)" lists only two values, but `af_tracking` in `crates/app/src/exif.rs:109` also maps 0 to `Off`, so every Sony frame shot without tracking shows `AF tracking: Off`. The parenthetical reads as an exhaustive list and suggests the row is absent when tracking is off. Either list all three (`Off`, `Face tracking` or `Lock On AF`), or reword it as examples (e.g. "the AF tracking, such as `Face tracking` or `Lock On AF`").
2. **docs/usage.md:99-100**: "the AF area (ILCE, NEX and ZV bodies only; other Sony families use a different table)" gives the wrong reason for part of the gate. The code comment on `af_area` (`crates/app/src/exif.rs:116-119`) says the `ILME-` bodies (e.g. FX3) and the RX/HX `DSC-` bodies use this *same* NEX/ILCE/ZV table and are left out deliberately (Trade-offs: "an ILME-FX3 or an RX100M7 shows no row"). Only SLT/HV/ILCA use a different table. An FX3 or RX100M7 user reading this doc would be told something untrue about why the row is missing. Drop the reason ("ILCE, NEX and ZV bodies only") or make it accurate (e.g. "ILCE, NEX and ZV bodies only; not the FX cinema bodies, the compacts, or the A-mount bodies").
