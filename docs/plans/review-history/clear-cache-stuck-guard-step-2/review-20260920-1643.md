### Findings

1. **crates/app/src/commands.rs:1004-1012 (`start_scan`)**: the emit of a constant `true` after `drop(state)` can land *after* the scan task's `false`, leaving the settings window stuck showing "scanning" forever — exactly the failure this feature exists to prevent. The task is spawned before `state.running` is stored, so it starts running immediately and its `finish` block only blocks on the `Scans` lock that `start_scan` still holds. As soon as `drop(state)` releases it, the task can take the lock, `finish(scan_id)`, compute `scanning() == false` (the `scan_folder` call that prepared this scan has already returned, so its `Preparing` is gone and `pending` was emptied by the `remove`) and emit `false` — all before `start_scan` reaches its own `publish_scan_state(&app, true)`. An empty or near-empty `todo` (`scan_folder` inserts a `PendingScan` even when `todo.len() == 0`) makes this window easy to hit. Fix by making the two emits ordered rather than racing, e.g. have `start_scan` publish the value recomputed under a freshly taken lock *after* the task can no longer be pending, or, simpler, emit the `true` before the task is spawnable — sequencing matters here, not just "which value"; emitting the value read under the lock at the moment of the store is not sufficient on its own.
